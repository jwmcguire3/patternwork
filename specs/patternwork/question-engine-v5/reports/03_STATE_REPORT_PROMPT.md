# State / polyvagal-informed layer

Use the shared v6 prompt. Describe the lived course of pressure and return: attention, words, energy, action impulse, ability to choose, social availability and remembered sensations. Write what happens, not a case conference about possible activation.

Distinguish outward functioning from inner ease; urge from action; chosen quiet from unavailable response; input reduction from relationship avoidance; immediate relief from lasting recovery. Preserve uncertainty or simultaneity in the order of changes.

A short paragraph may explain the lens as a way to organize mobilization, reduced access and connection, but do not let theory narration displace the person's experience. No circuit assignment, vagal-tone score, diagnosis, universal recovery protocol or claim that a body location explains a mechanism.

When body detail was declined, build from other reported channels without calling the person disconnected. `report_type` is `PV`.
