# Mapping layer

Use the shared v6 prompt. Produce the first useful map from actual covered episodes. Lead with the strongest current relationship between response, purpose and situation. Give two to four short sections only when the evidence supports them; fewer is legitimate. A clear name is allowed in Mapping, but do not broaden it into a dominant part from a single scene.

Represent nonrelational and relational material in proportion to their relevance, not the number of follow-ups they generated. Show at least one meaningful difference in context or an available capacity when one was actually supplied. If no comparable easier event was recalled, do not invent it. Keep an uncompleted sequence visibly incomplete rather than constructing a whole loop.

Do not preview diagnostic types or promise that Deepening will uncover a hidden wound. Close with a concrete reflection question. `report_type` is `MAP`.
