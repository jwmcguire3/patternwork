# Attachment layer

Use the shared v6 prompt. Explain the specific relationship patterns actually sampled. Keep seeking information, seeking reassurance, protesting hurt, wanting privacy, choosing autonomy, asking for care, receiving care and repair distinct where the data distinguish them.

Name anxious monitoring, guarded dependency, approach–withdrawal or protective distance when supported. Anchor the description to the right person and kinds of situations. A comparable case where the pattern did not appear is often central, not an inconvenient qualification.

Do not force an attachment explanation onto practical waiting, an unreliable situation, exhaustion or a justified limit. Do not infer a global style from one person. A sparse layer may plainly identify that only help-seeking was covered and omit unassessed romantic closeness; do not substitute hypothetical dating.
