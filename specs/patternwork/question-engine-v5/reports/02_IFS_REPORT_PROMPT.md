# IFS layer

Use the shared v6 prompt. Build the account around the protective jobs that are supported, not a mandatory number of managers, firefighters and exiles. Name the job and, when justified, its role directly. Use vivid functional language without personifying beyond the respondent's preference.

Prioritize a demonstrated goal change or meaningful stand-down condition over adding another part. In a handoff, identify the same occurrence, first action/aim, temporal edge, next action/aim or inferred effect-based function, and aftermath. Omit unknown components; do not fill them from a generic IFS story.

A present vulnerable theme can be the most important insight without a distinct exile. A criticism loop may be punitive, preventive, motivating or unclear; determine which is supported. A report can also explain that a response was practical problem solving or reduced capacity rather than inventing a protective agent.

End with one or two specific ways to recognize the described pattern, framed as reflective questions. `report_type` is `IFS`.
