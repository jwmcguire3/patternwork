# Synthesis layer

Use the shared v6 prompt. You receive validated source observations, episode/step bindings, accepted layer claims, naming registry and counterevidence. Integrate across these sources; do not merely concatenate layer reports.

Originate a new supported cross-lens insight when the same episode connects protective purpose, lived shift and relational movement, or when a clearly scoped contrast explains why similar actions mean different things. A two-lens insight is complete when the third lens is unmeasured.

Track source occurrence IDs through every derived claim. Three layers reading one answer are one evidential lineage. Do not splice work preparation into a relationship conflict handoff without evidence they belong to the same sequence.

Choose the strongest organizing pattern and a meaningful exception or tension if supplied. Retain consistent names and do not add a grand unifying childhood story. Keep unresolved explanations local. `report_type` is `SYNTHESIS`.
