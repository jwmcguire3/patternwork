# Interpretation contract — IFS system-portrait candidate 1

You receive a canonical PWRP/PWQE source-bound fictional evidence packet. Build an **internal, audit-ready** psychological account before writing any reader prose. The model's interpretation is not an empirical discovery of independently existing psychological entities.

## Build the functional graph

For each materially supported candidate response, decide:
- What kind of pressure or need brings it forward? Distinguish actual recalled occurrence, reported typicality, and unrelated situations.
- What is the response trying to accomplish? State a job supported by aim, timing, choice, effect, or converging evidence; a plausible effect alone is not automatically intent.
- What does it protect from, protect for, or make possible? Preserve practical, relational, contextual and ordinary alternatives.
- How does it operate? Preventive regulation and reactive interruption are different jobs. Involuntary loss of words/energy is not itself a choosing protector.
- What happens afterward? Tradeoff or relief only if actually reported. A sequence needs a sourced edge and a recurrence needs independently confirmed distinct episodes.
- How is it related to other parts or vulnerable experiences? Different roles are not automatically different entities; shared concern does not prove one permanent underlying part.
- Does the reader retain some choice, perspective, or capacity? Self-led findings require actual relational stance and available choice, not merely performance, calmness, or hindsight.

Produce supported claims with exact current observation IDs and source scope, evidence basis, dependencies, counterevidence and uncertainty. Preserve source options, status and occurrence identity. Treat synthetic Mapping scaffolds as fictional context, not corroboration of a person's life.

## Naming contract

Name the *function*, not the taxonomy. Favor a 1–2-word natural handle: Minimizer, Suppressor, Fixer, Critic, Watcher, Planner **only when supported**. Never select from this list as a classifier. Avoid “Don't-Be-a-Burden One”, “Load-Limiting Manager”, long explanatory phrases, forced personification, and dramatic invented personas.

Store stable `name_id` and `role` separately. A named role must be supported by the appropriate claim. The name can describe a protective behavior without proving a distinct enduring part identity. Use a consistent name throughout a report set; rename only through an explicit reviewed reconciliation. Do not force names on every vulnerability, ability, or local contrast. Exile is a role, not the name for every hidden need. “Self” is not a part name.

## Scope and uncertainty

The internal ledger, not the body prose, carries all meaningful validity limits. Distinguish:
- observed versus interpreted;
- singular occasion versus sampled recurring pattern;
- action versus aim versus consequence;
- internal access versus outward concealment;
- vulnerability versus internal exile;
- context/relational expectation versus the other person's true motives;
- capacity to function versus felt ease;
- reported order versus assumed handoff.

A strong locally grounded finding can be stated confidently in plain language. Do not turn a single occasion into a lifelong trait. An unanswered question means unknown, not absence. Keep apparently useful ordinary explanations as real findings.

## Output boundary

Return a structured interpretation ledger: claim records (with exact evidence IDs), candidate stable name records, explicit or withheld relationships, priority of findings, observed strengths and counterexamples, and confidence/scope notes. Mark hypotheses that cannot be established as `open_question` / `not_assessed`. Preserve PWRP v7 claim/relationship semantics; this is a proposed intermediate step, not a released draft. All resulting records require independent validation before composition.
