## What the report gets right

- **Function versus ease (CQ08):** M12, D80, D81, D83 and D85 support the report's distinction between carrying on, looking steady, feeling strained, and not yet feeling settled. It does not present practical function as recovery or comfort.
- **Reported order:** The D36 answer and two current sequence edges support reduced input before easier words before clearer thinking. The report preserves that partial order without claiming a causal mechanism or recurrence.
- **Partial easing and continuing concern (CQ09):** D37, D86 and D100 support partial easing in a quieter or less demanding setting and a concern that still made sense but felt less urgent. The report does not claim the concern vanished or recovery was complete.
- **Restraint and prose:** The prose stays within one fictional occurrence, leaves the trigger unknown, uses no unsupported IFS part names or physiology, and asks open questions without inserting assumed answers. It reads naturally, though the last section repeats earlier points.

## Meaningful omissions

1. **First easing time, moderate (CQ08):** D84 reports more than two hours but within the same day. Claim C10-C06 cites `O36b8ab6657f1876670d8`, but the rendered report omits this coarse estimate. The case requires the timing to remain an estimate, so leaving it out loses a positive distinction.
2. **Practical action versus setting, moderate (CQ09):** M11 reports an initial move somewhere with less input; M13 reports later time with less noise or fewer demands. Both authored observations were omitted from the curated graph when its contaminated C10-C03 claim was removed. The report retains D86's quieter setting but does not distinguish the respondent's actions from that later condition.
3. **Wanted speech versus word access, moderate:** D30 and D34 report knowing what to say and wanting to speak while being unable to get words out. The report says answering became harder and words later returned, but it misses the explicit capacity limit that helps distinguish involuntary difficulty from chosen withholding. Those observations were lost when the contaminated C10-C02 claim was omitted intact.

These are omissions, not unsupported additions. I found no material claim in the report that exceeds its cited authored evidence. The report is useful as a bounded diagnostic result, but its coverage is partial against the full packet.
