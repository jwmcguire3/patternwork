# Explaining, then seeking relief

*Patternwork is nonclinical self-reflection. Parts and state names are working interpretations, not diagnoses or physiological measurements.*

## Trying to influence what happens next

When the disagreement became tense, you kept explaining your point to influence what might happen next. Losing contact or not knowing where you stood was what concerned you most. In that moment, explaining may have served a preventive purpose; your stated aim was broader than avoiding one specific outcome.

## When explaining stops helping

When explaining no longer helped, the next move was to leave or end the exchange. You wanted the feeling to ease, and afterward it became easier to bear. Leaving or ending the exchange seems to have served that immediate need for relief.
