# C01/C02 reader-narrative controlled comparison

**Input:** First actual, accepted fictional IFS outputs provided for C01 and C02.

**Change surface:** Titles and consumer-facing prose, section order/claim placement, functional name labels, reflection prompt (C02 removed). The original validated claim graph, current observation references, role assignments, and relationship identities are frozen.

| Dimension | C01 original | C01 candidate | C02 original | C02 candidate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Central finding | Internal exclusion/exile, prevention, reactive push-away | One human narrative about what stays unasked and unallowed | Privacy, internal allowance and choice | Caring for another's capacity without abandoning one's own need |
| Names in reader prose | No stable functional names | Minimizer and Suppressor | Load-Limiting Manager | Minimizer |
| Reader-facing theory language | 'event-scoped Exile', 'Manager', 'Firefighter' | No IFS role jargon in prose | 'Manager', 'Self-led', 'Exile', 'unblending' | No IFS role jargon in prose |
| Help outcome | Offered help; need remained unspoken | Still offered, still unspoken | Offered help; remaining need expressed | Still offered, still expressed |
| Secondary contexts | Separate quasi-audit sections | Compressed, contextual closing section | Multiple detailed limits | Brief contrasts after the central story |
| Claims/evidence | Accepted original | Identical structured claims/evidence | Accepted original | Identical structured claims/evidence |

**What is established:** Both candidate artifacts were composed without provider calls; original artifact digests were verified; no claim, relationship, role, source identity, or evidence ID changed; frozen claims are all referenced in new sections. The local composer tests passed including both real uploaded outputs.

**What is not established:** The production TypeScript draft validator and a new semantic reviewer are not executable without the full repository/dependencies in the current environment. `verify-production.ts` is supplied for the repository. The reader previews are explicitly noncanonical layout variants. There is no evidence of improved comprehension from blinded consumer testing or reliability across other profiles.

**Recommended next experiment:** Pair original and candidate prose with no version labels, ask independent readers to identify the central pattern and C01/C02 distinction, and score recognition, clarity, individuality, overreach, and emotional tone. Avoid generalizing to other profiles until that comparison is complete.
