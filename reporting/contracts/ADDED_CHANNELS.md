# New evidence channels — PWRP 7.1 / PWQE 5.1

All new fields are current retrospective self-report, not measured anatomy or independent corroboration of the source episode. Use observations actually present, not the bank inventory.

D65–D66 describe a stance toward a reaction and room for choice. Curiosity alone, detachment alone, retrospective insight alone and calmness alone do not determine Self leadership. D67–D69 and D72–D74 distinguish internal admission, later access and actual effects from simply withholding a display. Exile-related formulation remains an accountable model interpretation, not a mandatory label or a keyword classifier.

D70–D71 describe the current force and revision of a self-conclusion. They do not report developmental acquisition. D75–D77 describe competing concerns, room for both and reported continuity across explicitly compared events. Their overlap does not independently prove shared part identity.

D78 records an actual later return, a return urge without action, no return, event ending or unclear memory. Only the actual return creates a new `return` step after `next`. D79 records what preceded that return; it is not automatically its unique cause. D99 records recalled typicality. Do not convert it into an additional independently sampled event or count it twice for recurrence.

D80–D83 cover retained capacity, outward/inward difference, onset shape and residual difficulty while functioning. D84 is a coarse estimate of time to first easing, not a precise threshold or physiological recovery time. D85 describes function/ease order. D86/D100 refer to the identified later recovery attempt, not automatically to the initial move. Changed conditions and changed meaning may accompany recovery without proving causation.

D87–D88 distinguish company effects from their later persistence or relief at solitude. D89–D90 are expectations remembered from the wait, not another person's actual motives. D91 records an actual response to an actual request; D92 separately records whether the need was met. A pending reply does not license a later-outcome account.

D93–D95 describe pace at the current stage of one connection and aftermath of interest actually shown. D96 distinguishes unfinished repair from inability to receive care. D97–D98 distinguish support for exploration, practical assistance, approval, privacy and retained decision-making. One episode does not establish a global attachment classification.

Use however many materially distinct supported findings are useful. No new channel requires a finding, a name, a section, or a theoretical balance. No unadministered question supplies a negative answer. Reviewer judgments remain evidence-linked and permit defensible alternative formulations.
