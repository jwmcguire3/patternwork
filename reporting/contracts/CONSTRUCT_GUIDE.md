# Construct guide: distinguish the models
Revision: formulation-first 1

IFS: protective purpose is an interpretation, not established by a behavior label. Manager prevention and firefighter relief are functions; their clock time is informative but not sufficient. A part is not its burden. A burden is a constraining/extreme belief or feeling it carries; ordinary fatigue, cost, and preference are not synonymous with burden. Exile requires a vulnerable affect/burden plus protective separation from internal contact. Infer this pattern when earned, without demanding a life-history explanation. A named Exile does not imply a known age, trauma, or origin. A vulnerable part or need may remain accessible and need no Exile label. Self is a way of relating in the model, not a named part or a score.

PV: lived activation/withdrawal/settling are model descriptions. Attention, language, energy, action, felt experience, and social contact may differ. Intention is not capacity; task persistence is not recovered ease. No questionnaire-derived vagal pathway or biological mechanism. Do not create a full ordered cycle from separate conditional responses.

ATT: distinguish needs, working expectations, signaling/withholding, relational conditions, and response to repair. Use context-specific dimensions and tendencies, not an immutable type or assumed caregiver history. Space can protect autonomy rather than signal avoidance; a desire for reassurance is not proof of dependency pathology.

Integration: classify statements before connecting them. Distinct lenses can explain different aspects without being equivalent or temporally linked. Source observations, not earlier prose, constrain claims. Separate inferred conceptual interaction from actual sequence. Consistent names are not proof of consistent mechanisms. Positive intent is not demonstrated benefit. No theory has to explain every observation.


## Exile inference and emotional development
An attention shift away from distress is not by itself a complete Exile formulation. Evaluate the particular present experience and the protective organization restricting internal contact with it; compare ordinary attentional rest, postponement and reduced capacity. This is an inference policy for a reflective report, not a diagnostic criterion. Do not require an origin story; do not fabricate one. When an Exile is earned, retain its role and develop its supported experience. Names remain ordinary handles separate from theoretical roles.
