# Interpretation guide — distinctions, not required output slots

This guide is for authors and reviewers. Active writer rules are in the prompts. These are qualitative product interpretation policies, not validated clinical cutoffs.

## Finding inclusion

Ask whether the interpretation adds materially distinct understanding and is supported at its claimed scope. Direct description, named roles, unnamed patterns, and well-scoped open questions can all be useful. No list of categories is a report checklist. No part-count score is defined.

## IFS

**Protective job:** what a response is trying to prevent, contain, or make bearable. Distinguish stated intention, inferred function, and actual outcome. Never infer benevolent motive solely from the IFS model's positive-intent orientation.

**Manager / Firefighter:** prevention versus reactive modulation of a difficult experience, not night-before versus during, cognition versus impulse, or good versus bad. Failure of relief does not erase an explicit relief-seeking job. Stillness may be limited capacity, not a protective choice.

**Vulnerable theme / vulnerable part / Exile:** the theme describes felt material; a part formulation organizes a recognizable current pattern; an exile formulation additionally concerns internal sequestration/exclusion/containment or protection against the system encountering it. External concealment can accompany exile but does not establish it alone. A theme can be vivid without a part; an exile can be named without a fabricated history.

**Burden:** a current constraining extreme belief/feeling carried by a part, when evidenced—not the part's essence and not every ordinary expectation. Acquisition history is not supplied by the label.

**Self / unblending:** describe a supported relation of perspective, openness, or choice toward one's experience. Do not assign a part identity, purity score, absence, or global trait. Capacity evidence may be useful without a Self claim. A calm-looking response is not decisive evidence.

**Part relations:** identity, coalition, opposition, shared protection, and handoff are separate interpretations. A shared word is not a shared exile; two wants are not automatically polarized; parallel strategies need not be one part. Document uncertain relations rather than completing a network.

## Attachment

Separate anxiety, avoidance/deactivation, secure functioning, current expectations, dependency, autonomy, care, repair, reassurance, and pace. Use the smallest accurate relationship scope; broader sampled patterns are allowed without pretending to produce validated classifications. Do not equate approach–withdrawal with infant disorganization or adult trauma classifications.

A practical boundary may be the accurate finding. Refusing poor repair is not necessarily rejection of care. Reassurance can help immediately without enduring; that does not invalidate a separately helpful repair. Private connection can grow faster than public expression without establishing lifetime bonding speed or rarity.

## State / polyvagal

Describe reported attention, language, energy, urge, sensations, and contact; relate them to model vocabulary when useful. A label does not measure a circuit. Strategy, emotion, bodily channel, and action availability are not interchangeable. Calm, Self-related capacity, and secure relational behavior require separate reasoning.

A gap between functioning and comfort is not automatically suppression; a body/behavior discrepancy does not automatically make the body the hidden truth. Preserve uncertainty, overlap, return-to-demand effects, and actual contextual constraints. No obligatory baseline, ladder, recovery duration, or exhausted-to-dorsal mapping.

## Synthesis

Trace same-occurrence relations separately from cross-context parallels. Interpretations from multiple reports share their underlying answers. A two-lens integration is legitimate; a third lens cannot be filled by association. Several independent processes can coexist. A loop needs evidence of a return. Disagreement between drafts must first be checked for an authoring error before being interpreted as human complexity.

## Review

False negatives matter, but omissions must be argued from the actual evidence, not from a preferred theory term. Distinct useful structures should not be merged for brevity; one structure should not be split for variety. Several reasonable formulations may preserve the same distinctions. Require fidelity and explanatory value, not one predetermined part count or name.
