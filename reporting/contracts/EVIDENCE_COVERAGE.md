It does not claim that a questionnaire can exhaust a person's internal life, validate the frameworks, reveal a developmental history, or measure physiology. ## Reachability, not compulsory completeness

Three new descriptive routing targets reuse D20, D22 and D23. A previous broader target could close after another useful question before one of these distinctions was collected. The new routes are anchored to relevant current answers; they do not force a part or condition a report on seeing a particular option.

The two new roots are **a reaction or wish kept private** (D72) and **trying something unfamiliar with or without support** (D97). They are available as user-selected topics. They are not appended to every Mapping pass.

## Deliberately outside the claim

No new questions collect trauma histories, childhood origin narratives, clinical diagnosis, vagal tone, medical explanations, a full Self score, exact thresholds, an exhaustive part graph, or lifetime prevalence. “Coverage” here is coverage of distinct report questions with authored neutral input paths—not confirmation that every possible report finding exists in every respondent.

The operational limits remain 56 substantive administrations and 72 decisions, with the existing lower-value scope/texture allowance. This bounds burden; it does not establish that average duration or the best stopping point is unchanged. The larger pool requires fresh respondent and provider qualification.
