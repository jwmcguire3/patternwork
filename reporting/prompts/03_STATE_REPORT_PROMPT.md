# State / polyvagal-informed report — revision 2

Use the shared contract. Set report_type to PV. Explain how lived state affects attention, language, thought, energy, sensation, action, social contact, and available choices. Let the actual movement and contrasts organize the report. Connect state, practical context, and available action where warranted; this explanatory account needs no protective motive or IFS cast.

Distinguish functioning from feeling settled, continuing a task from having flexibility, choosing not to speak from wanting to speak without words, and immediate relief from regained capacity. Mixed experiences belong together without claiming that one channel reveals the person's hidden truth. Do not infer dissociation, self-betrayal, or intentional suppression from a mismatch alone.

Use a simple state handle only when it helps. Polyvagal-informed terms can describe mobilization, settling, social engagement, shutdown-like withdrawal, or mixed experience within an explicit model frame. Do not claim measured vagal tone, a specific circuit, a biological baseline, or a mandatory ladder. Fawn is not a separate autonomic branch. Bodily location and intensity do not identify mechanism. Quietness can be rest, constraint, choice, or reduced capacity; effective action and excitement are not inherently dysregulation.

Preserve the source's temporal semantics. First noticed change, first easing, later improvement, regained words, and return of choice may be different. Unordered selections do not establish an order. Co-occurrence and noticing order are not causal precedence. Reported timing can matter without proving mechanism. No invented breaths, durations, times of day, body locations, or daily cycles.

Describe what helped and what remained difficult. Time, reduced input, movement, practical information, warmth, and another person's non-demanding presence may affect different channels. Help that requires explanation can increase load. Solitude is not automatically disconnection, and company is not automatically co-regulation. An association is not a universal remedy.

Describe the repertoire sampled rather than assigning a global nervous-system range. Missing recovery detail is not failed recovery. Declining body questions is not poor interoception. Symptoms remain descriptions, not proof of a psychological cause. Do not call a threat response a false alarm without evidence about actual conditions.

Close on distinctions and conditions that make the pattern recognizable. Do not add a nervous-system reset, breathing protocol, treatment advice, capacity quota, or compulsory hopeful ending.
