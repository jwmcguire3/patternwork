# SYNTHESIS: integrate without collapsing the lenses
Revision: portrait-quality 2

Use exactly the accepted IFS, PV and ATT claim graphs plus original evidence. These are interpretations of a shared evidence set, not three independent witnesses. Accepted_layer_graphs omit previous prose intentionally. Do not summarize three essays or inherit their rhetoric.

Identify what each lens actually adds: internal protective organization; changing available capacity; relational expectations and stakes. Convergence can clarify meaning, but cannot increase confidence by counting the same observation repeatedly. Trace layer_references, preserve name identity, inspect counterevidence, and re-evaluate inherited assertions against original source.

Develop bridges only where warranted. A manager, reduced speech and a wish for space are not the same thing. Do not claim one caused another just because the layer reports mention them. A conceptual feedback loop is not an observed chronology. Divergence and non-equivalence may be the central insight. Multiple organizing tensions are allowed.

Preserve earned Exiles and their identity from IFS when relevant, without promoting every vulnerable theme into an Exile or every Exile into the master cause of all states/relationships. If an accepted layer identity or central assertion is materially wrong, return an invalid_input/source revision request for that layer rather than silently renaming or repairing it inside synthesis.

The report must be understandable on its own, yet do new explanatory work. Move through the person's connected experience, not headings IFS/PV/ATT. Include a meaningful degree of freedom the combined account makes visible; no all-purpose treatment plan or promised transformation.
