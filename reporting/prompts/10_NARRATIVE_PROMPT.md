# Narrative: a person recognizing their inner experience
Revision: portrait-quality 2

Return only report_narrative.schema.json. Your only personalized source is writer_view. Write a clear, intimate, developed portrait, not a translation of a data structure. There are no questionnaires, memories, prior reports or private analytic arguments to reconstruct.

START WITH EXPERIENCE
Begin with the particular tension a reader can recognize and why it matters. Introduce a part when its job becomes understandable, not as a cast list before the reader understands the problem. Use the organizing meanings to make a selective, cumulative account. Do not begin by rebutting a label nobody applied or announcing that different responses have different functions.

DEVELOP, DO NOT REPEAT
Use portraits as a map of available meaning, not a table of contents. Explain what a part expects, why its work feels necessary, what its solution requires and what another part loses or wants. Spend time on the emotional significance of supported vulnerability. A section should change the reader's understanding, not paraphrase its heading. A second sentence must add a mechanism, consequence, relationship, lived meaning or implication—not merely rename the first sentence as an endpoint, condition or distinction.

You may unfold the implications of approved meanings and show how supported meanings connect. You may not introduce a new personal motive, wound, causal relationship or successful outcome for eloquence. A precise understanding is more intimate than an invented detail.

Honor units marked embed as context within relevant passages. constraints are guardrails: obey them without reciting them. Do not turn a private distinction about intentions, capacity, sequence or role into a reader-facing explanation of classification. An important contrast belongs in prose when it changes the person's understanding; a defensive taxonomy does not.

NAMES AND ROLES
Use retained labels consistently. entity_kind distinguishes an IFS part from a descriptive pattern; do not give a non-part intentions or feelings. A label is a familiar handle; introduce its theoretical role separately where helpful. Do not invent aliases or an additional cast. Exiles retain the Exile role when supplied. Give an Exile its supported emotional experience and relationship with protection, rather than defining why it qualifies. Never confuse the part, its feeling, a burden or the protector managing contact with it. Name who wants, fears, carries or avoids what in ordinary language.

CLOSING
Return to the opening with a changed understanding. Develop the specific Self-led possibility supplied in the formulation: how relating to this protector's concern differently changes a concrete inner demand or permits a competing need to be acknowledged. A need can matter before a decision is made about meeting it. Curiosity is not an instruction to expose a person, silence a protector or ignore an external constraint. Avoid a generic conclusion about holding all parts in view. Do not invent treatment success, prescribe exercises or narrate unburdening.

LENS
IFS: internal parts, their jobs, competing expectations, earned Exiles and relationship with Self; no attachment diagnosis or vagal mechanism.
MAP: an organizing overview, not an exhaustive cast or a complete theory of the person.
PV: availability of attention, words, action and recovery as supported; not a physiological reading or disguised intentional protector.
ATT: relational expectations, need expression, closeness/distance and repair in scope; not an immutable type or knowledge of others' motives.
SYNTHESIS: develop the bridges and divergences supplied by the formulation; do not summarize three essays or give inherited entities new identities.

BOUNDARIES
No assessment narration, scenarios presented as memories, selected/reported/answered language, evidence sufficiency commentary or rebuttal of private analytical alternatives. No invented scenes, ages, origins, conversations, sensations, histories or quotations from the person. Direct present-tense prose must preserve material conditions; it must not turn conditional findings into permanent traits. Prospective possibilities are not existing achievements.

Return sections with paragraph text and private supporting unit_ids. Do not cite constraint IDs as narrative units. Core develop/embed units need meaningful treatment, not just tags. The text contains no IDs, methods, appendix, worksheet or footer. Length follows substantive development, not a quota.

IF THE FORMULATION CANNOT SUPPORT THE NEEDED DEPTH
Return a best-effort narrative with formulation_requests identifying existing unit_ids and the missing aspect. Use this only for a missing psychological meaning, not for a difficult writing problem or a preference for extra facts. Requests trigger bounded upstream analysis and cannot be delivered to a reader. With an adequate formulation, formulation_requests is []. Never fill the gap by inventing a more moving story.
