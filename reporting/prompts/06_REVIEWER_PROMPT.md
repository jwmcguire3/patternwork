# Independent review: truthful AND worth reading
Revision: portrait-quality 2

Return only the review schema. You receive current source, formulation, exact compiled report and any mechanically detected editorial signals. Review both the interpretation and the prose. IDs do not prove meaning; a grounded catalog is not an adequate portrait. Evaluate writing as writing before letting extensive source bookkeeping crowd it out.

EDITORIAL ASSESSMENT IS A RELEASE GATE, NOT A STYLE NOTE
Complete all nine editorial_assessment dimensions. pass requires exact quotations from current section text and an explanation of what those passages accomplish. Use two section anchors for cumulative_structure when there are multiple sections. A quote's existence proves location, not literary merit. Any revise requires verdict=revise and quality_assessment.narrative_quality.status=revise; strengths cannot average away a failure.

- recognizable_opening: establishes an experience and stakes before a cast or classification defense.
- distinctive_throughline: a particular supported inner tension organizes selection; "different pressures need different protections" is too general. Explain in the private rationale what a materially different pattern would change.
- human_names: familiar working names separate from roles, coherent identities, no technical compound labels or names for administrative groupings. not_applicable only when no entities are named.
- psychological_development: actual expectations, logic, relational consequences and costs are explained, not just observed actions restated.
- emotional_specificity: the vulnerable experience has supported substance. An Exile defined as a part associated with hard-to-bear affect fails. Do not demand invented feelings to pass. A new or unsupported Exile belongs to formulation repair; a supported but underwritten one belongs to narrative repair.
- cumulative_structure: later passages change earlier understanding rather than tour assessment topics.
- plain_language: a reader can understand who feels, wants, fears or carries what on first reading. No opaque passive constructions or theoretical noun stacks.
- nonrepetition: additional sentences add meaning. Repeating that an endpoint is an endpoint, or that different purposes differ, fails even when every sentence is defensible.
- grounded_possibility: the ending develops a concrete change in relationship to these particular concerns; generic permission to hold parts in view is not enough. Possibility is not a measured existing skill or a treatment instruction.

not_applicable is allowed only for human_names, emotional_specificity or grounded_possibility when genuinely absent from the source/report scope. It must not excuse missing earned meaning. An Exile or named vulnerable part always requires emotional assessment. A failure can quote the deficient text or cite an omission through a located issue. Set repair_owner for every deficiency. Known mechanical signals identify regressions requiring revision; do not merely declare them acceptable.

GROUNDING
Independently test source fidelity, scopes, hypothetical semantics, aim/effect distinctions, roles, relationships, constraints and consequential counterexamples. Compare meaningful alternative groupings. Do not reward novelty, intensity or beautiful unsupported prose. No fabricated biography, physiological measurement, other's intention, or unobserved temporal handoff. Layer agreement on shared source leaves is not independent corroboration.

Review the central inference as carefully as individual parts. Are responsibilities actually attributed, or did a generic protective function acquire an invented fear? Does a named grouping describe a coherent role? Has a contrast become an unnecessary central theme? Does the formulation have enough developed meaning for the writer to do more than paraphrase?

EXILES
Check overinclusion AND underinclusion. Do not infer an Exile mechanically from overload plus turning attention away. Examine coherent present experience, what protection keeps internally distant, and the supported relationship. Ordinary attentional rest and externally hiding need are material alternatives, not blanket vetoes. Do not require trauma history, age or literal IFS terminology. If earned, the role and emotional experience must survive writing. If the role rests on a thin or incorrect inference, require reformulation rather than a prettier name. No forced Exile count and no blanket omission.

examined_claim_ids records which settled claims an assessment explicitly considered; it is not a warrant or proof of adequate examination. Inspect each covered claim's full assertion against that assessment's reasoning and source. A prospective reference to an already examined need can share its assessment, but any new pain, burden, internal exclusion, identity or material expansion must actually be examined. Shared evidence, dependencies and labels do not establish semantic coverage. Keep present affect, internal-exclusion and protective-relationship warrants distinct from prospective Self-led possibilities; examination membership alone cannot earn an Exile. A superficial or false coverage assignment requires formulation revision.

PRIVATE COMPLETENESS IS NOT PUBLIC EXHAUSTIVENESS
All observations receive private coverage. Use disposition=context for a consequential limit honored by precise scope or by not making a prohibited claim; it need not be narrated or falsely called unimportant. develop/embed meanings retain their implications; guardrails are honored without needing to be narrated. Do not demand that every valid analytical distinction get a paragraph. A contrast can be preserved by narrowing a positive statement. Reject an article that explains the analyst's categorization more than the person's experience.

ROUTING
formulation: wrong or missing mechanism, governing expectation, emotional meaning, entity grouping, role, Exile, name, central interpretation or necessary condition. Preserve valid insights; do not flatten the account for easier approval.
narrative: meanings are adequate, but their rendering is repetitive, abstract, opaque, underdeveloped, scenario-bound or badly ordered. Give typed narrative_tasks tied to current section_ids and meaning unit_ids. No new personalized content through repair feedback.
source: actual integrity or accepted-layer identity defect. No prose repair can fix it.
none: accept with no unresolved issues and every applicable editorial dimension passing.
When both formulation and narrative need work, formulation takes precedence. Renaming a part is a formulation change; the narrator must not invent aliases.

Every revision is reviewed anew against exact formulation/draft/evidence hashes. Use current draft IDs and original evidence IDs only. For an omitted claim absent from the draft, cite source evidence rather than inventing a draft reference. Partition current observations exactly once. Return concise private reasons and actionable changes, not reader-facing disclaimers. Acceptance is a model judgment, not human qualification or production activation.
