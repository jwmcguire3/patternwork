# Attachment report — revision 2

Use the shared contract. Set report_type to ATT. Describe how connection, separateness, dependence, uncertainty, receiving care, and repair operate in the relationships actually sampled. Write a recognizable account of expectations and moves, not a generic attachment-style profile.

Infer a present working expectation when the evidence supports it: what you anticipate about someone's availability, the cost of needing, your acceptability, or the likelihood that repair will hold. Keep its context and warrant. An organizing expectation does not establish childhood parenting, an attachment injury, a hidden memory, or an immutable identity.

Attachment anxiety, hyperactivating pursuit or monitoring, avoidance or deactivation, and secure functioning may help explain a supported pattern. Introduce terminology plainly and keep lived experience primary. Terms are neither banned nor required. Do not equate assessment traditions, produce a validated global score, or force a partner/friend/family comparison.

Distinguish seeking information from seeking reassurance, expressing hurt from trying to restore contact, and reaching for connection from trying to end internal tension. Rest, privacy, lower interest, practical stakes, real unreliability, and justified boundaries remain available explanations. The account gives the respondent's experience, not access to another person's motives.

Where timing is established, explain what the first move tries to do, what an unanswered move changes, and whether the next response has a different purpose. Approach followed by withdrawal does not alone establish disorganization, fearful-avoidant identity, protest, collapse, or trauma.

Preserve distinctions among asking for help, receiving help, offering repair, receiving adequate repair, immediate reassurance, its durability, and later contact. Refusing inadequate repair is not an inability to receive love. A contrast is meaningful only when the source supports the comparison.

Keep privately developing importance, publicly shown interest, readiness to disclose, and readiness to reconnect distinct. D52/D53 can support an internal/external pace difference in the sampled connection, not lifetime rarity of bonding, an exact threshold, or a universal ideal speed.

Describe secure functioning through contextual capacities such as realistic reliance, direct needs, separateness without retaliation, credible repair, and continued responsiveness under tension. Appearing calm or accommodating someone is not automatically security. Distance and a clear no can be part of secure functioning.

Show what is different when trust, predictability, sufficient room, or adequate repair is present without turning the association into advice. No quota for insecure findings, secure findings, asymmetries, or questions. End at the supported understanding rather than a prescribed relationship decision.
