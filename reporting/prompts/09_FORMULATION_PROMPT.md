# Formulation: understand the person before planning the article
Revision: portrait-quality 3, explicit examination coverage

Return only report_formulation.schema.json. This is an analytical artifact, not the reader's report. Use current evidence and the selected lens; previous report prose is not evidence. Record concise conclusions and warrants, not a transcript of deliberation.

FIRST FIND WHAT MATTERS
Find the most distinctive, well-supported inner tension. Ask what the person is trying to preserve, what another need or condition makes difficult, and what this arrangement costs. Compare plausible organizing accounts privately. Choose the one that makes the strongest useful connection without swallowing exceptions. "Different responses have different purposes" is an analytical caution, not an organizing portrait. A coherent account need not be a complete theory of the person.

Separate data from inference: response, stated aim, expectation, effect, context, capacity limit and counterexample. Preserve authored referents and timing. Hypothetical situations are conditional evidence, never personal history. Expectations are not other people's actions; protective intentions are not successful outcomes. Use these distinctions to improve conclusions, not to fill the article with classification defenses.

DEVELOP MEANING, NOT ONLY LABELS
Group by protective/adaptive function before naming. Examine the cost of combining or separating patterns. Do not create one part per item or collapse distinct relief strategies into one actor. Infer a function or conceptual relationship when the whole evidence makes it a defensible explanation; the reader need not have supplied its literal wording. Conversely, an interesting possible motive is not automatically the person's motive.

For each central process, develop the expectation that makes it necessary, what changes when it takes priority, and the personal cost or wanted experience. A stopping cue is only a starting point: examine what relying on that cue means. Add earned explanatory claims and meaning units. Do not merely say the same observation twice. Preserve good alternatives and genuinely unresolved candidates privately.

MEANING UNITS HAVE DIFFERENT DELIVERY JOBS
- develop: a substantive insight whose logic, emotional meaning or consequence deserves prose.
- embed: a consequential condition or contrast woven into the insight it changes; not automatically a separate section.
- guardrail: a limit against a misreading. It constrains the writer but is not a compulsory topic or citable narrative unit. Example: unavailable words must not be redescribed as a deliberate refusal. That instruction does not require a paragraph defending the difference.

importance=core means omission changes the portrait. Do not mark every observation, contrast or caveat core. guardrail units must be supporting. Preserve meaningful contrary evidence in the actual conclusion, not by presenting a universal claim and appending a caveat. Units and development purposes must be scenario-free, present-tense, reader-safe meanings. Claim IDs, raw wording, scope accounting and alternative explanations stay private.

PART PORTRAITS (IFS)
Keep name_registry.name a short, ordinary working name; keep manager/firefighter/exile/protective_part in role. A label is a handle, not a theory definition. Do not use technical compound names such as "Low-Input Relief Protector" or "Overload-Vulnerable Part." Do not personify an administrative grouping with no coherent inner job.

For every retained IFS part, populate portraits with references to units explaining its job, governing expectation, feared outcome, cost, felt experience, longing, release condition and a possible Self-led relationship. Use only earned facets. Empty facets are allowed; do not fabricate a fear, feeling or longing to complete the form. When a central facet is missing, examine the evidence for a defensible inference before leaving it empty. release_condition can describe an observed condition or a clearly prospective possibility, not a guaranteed remedy. self_relationship units are possibilities, not measured abilities.

RELATIONSHIPS
A relationship unit should explain what each side preserves and how their approaches constrain, reinforce or protect one another. "They conflict" is a finding, not its development. Preserve the difference between conceptual interaction and a documented timed handoff. Don't invent an escalation sequence to make the parts connect.

EXILES: EXAMINE ACTIVELY AND IN BOTH DIRECTIONS
Exiles are important and must be named and emotionally developed when earned. Examine each meaningful vulnerability cluster, including where protection makes an important experience difficult to acknowledge internally. Present burden, coherent internal separation and protective relationships can earn the model without a known age, childhood story or explicit IFS vocabulary.

For each assessment, list every settled claim you have considered in examined_claim_ids. Every reported or supported_interpretation claim tagged vulnerable_theme, vulnerable_part, burden, internal_exclusion or exile must appear in at least one such list. Include later derived claims and prospective Self-led references explicitly; dependencies do not supply coverage. When a later claim refers only to the same already-examined need, add its ID to the matching assessment and ensure that assessment's reasoning covers its whole assertion. Do not invent another Exile or repeat the same assessment merely to cover a reference. New affect, burden, internal exclusion, vulnerable identity or material expansion requires its own examination; a repeated label or shared evidence does not establish that it was already examined.

Keep examination coverage separate from warrants. affect_claim_ids, exclusion_claim_ids and relationship_claim_ids retain their present affect/need, internal-exclusion and protective-relationship meanings; each warrant ID must also appear in the same assessment's examined_claim_ids. A clearly prospective relationship with an acknowledged need may be examined without supplying any of those present warrants. For example, recognizing a need without treating a protector's prediction as fact can refer to an examined cluster without proving internal exclusion or a present protective relationship. Never put a prospective claim into a present-warrant array merely to satisfy coverage. Listing an ID asserts examination; it does not prove the reasoning or earn an Exile.

Do not convert "distress + attention shifted away" mechanically into an Exile. Compare internal exclusion with ordinary attentional rest, reduced input, deliberate postponement and unavailable capacity. Identify what experience the part carries, what protection keeps away from awareness or acceptance, and how those meanings relate. A generic "hard-to-bear affect" and a new role label do not complete that reasoning. A role warrant and an emotional portrait are different responsibilities.

Do not force specificity by inventing loneliness, shame, abandonment, trauma or an unmet childhood need. Equally, do not suppress a well-supported Exile because origins are unavailable. Record the decision and its concise warrant privately. With earned_exile, register the Exile, retain its protective relationship, and provide a core developed experience unit referenced by its portrait. A supported emotional experience deserves attention whether or not a distinct Exile is earned.

COMPOSITION PLAN
organizing_unit_ids should identify the central lived tension and meaning, not an inventory of differences. Use development_order to build understanding: recognition, protective logic, interaction, vulnerable stakes and a more discriminating possibility, in whatever order fits. Each entry's reframes_unit_ids identifies earlier meanings whose significance this step changes. Empty is appropriate for an initial or independent step. Later sections must add understanding, not new assessment topics. Omit peripheral material from prose when its absence does not distort the account; retain its private coverage.

SOURCE AND OTHER LENSES
Use current IDs, exact source bindings, honest dependencies and all consequential contrary leaves. Preserve actual scope and timing. Never name Self as a part. For MAP, PV, ATT and SYNTHESIS, use the selected lens's mechanisms; portraits can be empty when no IFS parts are authored. Synthesis uses the exact accepted layer graphs and does not manufacture independent corroboration or silently change inherited identities. Coverage partitions every current observation once without requiring every observation in the article.
